# Journey exploration delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Contextual event meaning and graph highlights
The owner SHALL configure discovered events through a node popup with real-record preview and prospective activation preserving other mappings. Unmapped nodes SHALL remain unconnected absent evidence. The graph SHALL offer rare-path, endings, warning/exception and recovery lenses with evidence drilldown. Rarity SHALL use comparable full closed, non-partial, uncensored sequences; warnings SHALL NOT terminate journeys.

#### Scenario: Configure one discovered event
- **WHEN** the owner opens its popup, previews and activates a mapping
- **THEN** all other mappings remain, captured journeys keep their definition and app attribution remains receiver-validated and observations retain their untrusted assurance

#### Scenario: Rare sequence support
- **WHEN** a full sequence has at most ten percent share among at least twenty closed, non-partial, uncensored journeys with the same definition and initial version
- **THEN** the rare lens may highlight it with counts/support, without implying its links occur exclusively in rare paths or that rarity proves a defect

#### Scenario: Named ending states
- **WHEN** endings are highlighted
- **THEN** pending, inactive, explicit exit and completed outcomes remain distinguishable

#### Scenario: Clear a selected visit
- **WHEN** the owner uses the X on the selected journey/visit filter
- **THEN** the workspace returns to all traffic under its other filters and layout without deleting evidence

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Role-free client investigation
The active workspace SHALL expose no browser/server selector, trusted-producer control, server-only journey destination or backend dependency analysis. Service, signal/carrier, version, definition, time, visit/tab and evidence filters SHALL remain available where supported. Role removal SHALL preserve useful client journey, timeline, diagnostic and trace investigation.

#### Scenario: Configure and investigate a client event
- **WHEN** an owner opens a discovered event, previews a mapping and follows a matching journey
- **THEN** service/carrier/attribute context disambiguates the event without a role choice and the journey supports evidence, diagnostic detail and a return to the originating question

#### Scenario: Existing compact tools and views
- **WHEN** an owner or temporary viewer uses the client workspace in English or French at desktop, 390 CSS pixels or 200% zoom
- **THEN** Tools/Outils, all seven direct journey views, keyboard/focus/dismissal behavior and stable graph presentation remain available within the existing compact header budgets
- **AND** server-role removal adds no permanent header rows or dead-end controls

#### Scenario: Retired role filter or old reference
- **WHEN** an API query or saved reference depends on removed role semantics or non-comparable legacy evidence
- **THEN** the API reports an explicit unsupported compatibility state rather than ignoring the filter or widening results
- **AND** the UI explains the limit and offers return, clear-filter or current-client-period selection without overwriting the saved reference

#### Scenario: Live and historical context
- **WHEN** the viewer changes between historical and live client populations
- **THEN** existing bounds, baseline separation, occurrence/receipt times, partial evidence and retention warnings remain explicit, with no implicit mixing of legacy history
